# Phase 1 comparison and proposed merge

3 October 2026. Status: **recommendations for the next revision**. This compares the supplied second-model report, matrix and curriculum with our phase-1 outputs. It does not complete the legacy audit or certify any pilot entry. Neither draft's proposed ranking has been validated against learner outcomes.

## Recommendation

Use our downloaded-source evidence and separation of level, usefulness and task eligibility as the foundation. Add the other draft's Cornetto/ODWN morphology, Apertium corroboration, explicit NT2Lex quality checks, example-vocabulary checks and additional starter-fixture cases. Retain SUBTLEX-NL as the primary frequency input. Treat the alternative size targets and numerical ranking calibration as hypotheses.

**The main correction to my earlier report is substantive:** ODWN is useful for grammar corroboration. My statement that it was not a grammar authority obscured its valuable Cornetto/RBN lexical layer. It should have distinguished that layer from automatically added senses and links.

## Changes needed before adopting the grammar proposal

**Preserve field and source lineage.** `cdb2.2_Auto` is present on entries with useful RBN morphology; rejecting every record whose provenance contains “Auto” would discard valid source information. Conversely, a “Manual” sense/link label does not certify every grammatical field. Some provenance strings also include Wiktionary, so source independence must be assessed for the particular evidence being compared.

**Check internal consistency.** The inspected ODWN `huis-n-1` has singular article `het`, while its `MorphoSyntax` gender attribute is `m_f`. Treat that as a source-field inconsistency, not two independent votes. Prefer an explicitly supported article observation and preserve the conflict for review.

**Retain usage notes from the original page.** The inspected Kaikki `werken` record contains both modern and archaic conjugation tables. Its ordinary `wrocht` and `gewrocht` form rows lack an archaic tag, although the original page explicitly labels the whole second paradigm archaic/obsolete. Simple filtering of form tags would miss this. Preserve table context or attach a sourced override to affected forms before activating production tasks.

**Store conditional article alternatives.** [Taaladvies.net](https://taaladvies.net/welk-of-welke-soort/) permits both articles for `soort` meaning category, but specifies `de` for the biological species meaning. This also shows an incompleteness in the English-Wiktionary snapshot, which presents the category entry as neuter only. [Onze Taal](https://onzetaal.nl/taalloket/de-het-deksel) explicitly accepts both articles for `deksel`. These are useful adjudication sources. Do not turn “all four legacy risk words allow both” into a blanket grading rule without checking the intended sense and construction.

The requirement “Kaikki plus an independent lexicon for every grammar fact, or flagged” is a useful aspiration, but coverage differs by field. Apertium does not verify IPA; ODWN does not provide every form. Phase 3 should state field-specific evidence requirements, the meaning of a single-source verification, escalation conditions, and task eligibility. Missing corroboration should remain visible. A second model's agreement is never a replacement dictionary observation.

## Curriculum: merge the structure, revise the rules

The other draft's frequency-to-level formula does not yet establish calibrated difficulty. Its corpus-level medians can describe an association, but the supplied interquartile ranges overlap. Estimating on the same NT2Lex labels also inherits their sampling and annotation problems. Filling fixed capacities after usefulness adjustments makes “level” depend on what else happens to be in the candidate pool.

Our 35/25/20/10/10 priority weights are also unvalidated editorial choices. Retain their transparency and component logging; do not represent them as empirically optimal.

Recommended merged decisions:

1. Keep four separate fields: source level evidence, reviewed `estimated_cefr`, `curriculum_stage`/introduction priority, and task release eligibility. A useful administrative term can be introduced early without being relabelled linguistically easy.
2. Use SUBTLEX's provided lemma/POS frequency fields as primary, with contextual diversity and mapping caveats. Keep wordfreq as a sensitivity check. Summing unique, unambiguous surface probabilities can be an approximate fallback; it is not an observed lemma count. Homographs, lexicalised diminutives, duplicated forms, blended corpora and split separable verbs need explicit handling.
3. Keep our cumulative **600 / 1,500 / 3,000 / 5,000** sense-entry budgets for this revision. Record **600 / 2,000 / 3,500 / 5,000** as the alternative to compare after candidate selection. Neither set is a CEFR requirement. The [publisher sample](https://www.nt2.nl/media/60/9789046907412_inkijk_woorden_in_context_1-6.pdf) does cite approximately 2,000 _words_ for A2, but does not establish equivalence to our sense-and-expression counting unit. It justifies reconsideration, not an automatic conversion.
4. Adopt scenario units and gradual reintroduction of themes. Add an example check allowing the target, already introduced senses/constructions, an explicitly taught scaffold, and at most one glossed unknown lexical item. A form index supplies possible analyses; it cannot resolve every homograph, compound, separable construction or unfamiliar grammatical pattern. Flag uncertain analyses. “Transparent to an English/Polish speaker” is a reviewed hypothesis, not proof that the learner knows it.
5. Separate confusable senses and semantic siblings, but keep “14 study days”, “one band later” and “at most two related words” configurable. The draft provides no evidence for those exact thresholds. Permit useful contrasting constructions when the learner's needs and existing knowledge support them.
6. Use placement to distinguish prior knowledge from study completion. Cognate status may suggest placement candidates; it must not automatically mark them mastered. Consider receptive-first enrollment individually, rather than disabling production for every B2 word by default.
7. Keep an explicit exception list for essential named services such as DigiD. The other curriculum both adds DigiD and excludes brands/proper names. Similarly, OpenTaal absence should trigger review rather than automatically exclude a valid compound, abbreviation or expression.
8. Split senses when definitions, usage, grammar or grading need the distinction. A difference in English or Polish translations is a helpful signal, not a sufficient definition of a new Dutch sense. Do not derive an abbreviation's article solely from an expanded phrase without documenting that inference or finding attestation.
9. Keep everyday institutions and civic interactions in the core. Specialist constitutional/history material can be opt-in; moving the entire “state/law” domain out would be too broad. Add IT or Flemish packs only as optional proposals; the user's household profession was not specified.
10. Generate Polish directly from the intended Dutch sense and Dutch example, using Polish lexical evidence where it matches. Review EN and PL against the same Dutch source. Routing NL through an English translation risks propagating an English sense error.

## Evidence and remaining limits

Read in full: the supplied `phase-1-sources-and-selection.md`, `source-matrix(1).md`, `curriculum(1).md`, the supplied copy of our `source-matrix.md`, and our report/curriculum. The bundle records hashes of these inputs.

Reused pinned data include [NT2Lex basic](https://cental.uclouvain.be/cefrlex/static/resources/nl/NT2Lex-CGN-v01.tsv), [NT2Lex sense-linked](https://cental.uclouvain.be/cefrlex/static/resources/nl/NT2Lex-CGN%2BODWN-v01.tsv), [ODWN v1.4](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/cltl/OpenDutchWordnet/master/resources/odwn/odwn_orbn_gwg-LMF_1.4.xml.gz), and the earlier inspected Kaikki/SUBTLEX/Tatoeba snapshots. The [ODWN project documentation](https://github.com/cltl/OpenDutchWordnet) describes its RBN layer and release history. [wordfreq's maintainer documentation](https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq) describes its multi-source estimates and approximately 2021 data cutoff. Piper [current voice documentation](https://github.com/OHF-Voice/piper1-gpl/blob/main/docs/VOICES.md) and the [specific voice list](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper/blob/master/VOICES.md) were opened; audio was not generated or listened to.

Not validated here: the other model's full ranked list, wordfreq calibration cohorts, per-ID noun audit, claimed classification of most article disagreements, full source independence, actual audio quality, or the learning benefits of either proposed curriculum. This comparison supplies corrections and merge decisions; the research remains paused before phase 2.
