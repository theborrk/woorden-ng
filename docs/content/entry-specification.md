# Content entry specification — draft 0.2

This proposal implements blueprint §§7.1, 10 and 14 while keeping content preparation outside the app. A finished learning entry represents **one intended sense or conventional construction**. `research/content-2026-10/content/starter-pack.json` contains 60 populated research drafts in this format; it is not an approved learning pack. Unknown or inapplicable facts remain explicit. Fields present with a null value do not count as content coverage.

## Identity and granularity

Allocate opaque UUIDs once and commit `research/content-2026-10/content/id-registry.json`. Its human-readable keys are allocation labels, not an algorithm to regenerate IDs after an edit. Keep the issued UUID when spelling, translation or ordering changes. A genuinely new sense receives a new sense ID. The two bank senses share a lexeme ID and have different sense IDs; the two pragmatic uses of alsjeblieft do likewise. Homographs with different pronunciation/morphology, such as the two voorkomen verbs, need separate lexeme records or explicitly distinct homograph variants.

The JSON is a denormalized exchange view. The production compiler extracts canonical Lexeme, Sense, Form, Example, Rule and Media records and rejects contradictory copies of a shared entity. Store article/morphology at lexeme or form scope and countability, use restrictions, translations and task eligibility at sense/construction scope. Never derive de/het from Polish gender or from a person's gender. A source tag `m_f` is not a safe substitute for an explicit article field.

## Provenance resolution in the exchange format

`provenance` keys are JSON-pointer **prefixes**. A more specific pointer overrides its parent; source references directly on a form/IPA item override the enclosing group. `/sense` therefore covers its generated definition, meaning, register and usage notes unless a child has its own provenance. Source quotations/glosses remain observations and do not make the authored meanings source-verified. Bookkeeping fields (`id`, `revision`, `content_sha256`, registry links) are deterministic/allocation metadata; preserve their registry/compiler provenance. The accompanying validator checks references and structure; it does not infer truth from a URL.

The pilot retains source morphology at lexeme scope. Production normalization must move sense-dependent `applies_to_selected_sense` restrictions to the sense/form link, not duplicate incompatible lexeme records. A form list is an evidence inventory: `grading_enabled:false` throughout this draft. For productive/cloze trials, the source form plus the sentence, translation, accepted segments and ambiguity review must all pass.

## Completion and task eligibility

An entry is finished **for a named task**, not necessarily for every possible mode. Receptive meaning practice needs a scoped reviewed meaning and adequate cue; an article task additionally needs sourced article data; a form task needs the specified form and construction; an example task needs reviewed NL/EN/PL and valid spans; listening needs tested audio; picture naming needs a reviewed image and accessible alternative. Missing optional IPA does not disable a written meaning task. Missing PL disables PL-dependent prompts or invokes an explicitly selected EN fallback (T43), never silently generated PL.

The starter drafts have no eligible curated tasks yet because independent vendor review has not run. Structural checks and source extraction have run. This distinction is deliberate and testable under T50.
