## Goal

A contributor can inspect a typed response against an explicit sense/form contract and distinguish target success, article error, valid alternative and unsafe typo acceptance.

## Context

- Blueprint §§10.2–10.3 and T10–T12; ADR 0005.
- Consume T-110 entry contracts/issued IDs; no new dictionary or accepted-answer authoring pipeline.

## Scope

In:

- Pure typed evaluator and direct Node inspector for Unicode/whitespace/permitted punctuation normalization and explicit accepted forms.
- Separate lexical, meaning, article and spelling components; known real-word/form alternatives are never silently accepted by edit distance.
- Ambiguous valid alternative gives ungradable target with repair reason; precise mismatches remain errors.
- Receptive free-text unsupported by reviewed equivalents returns self-assessment-required instead of invented exact grading.

Out (do not do in this task):

- Runtime dictionary/network lookup, unrestricted fuzzy matching, content review, UI or scheduler updates.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] AC1: Given accepted forms for a specific sense and permitted normalization, when evaluated, then equivalent spacing/Unicode/punctuation matches without collapsing meaningful accents or endings globally (unit and integration).
- [ ] AC2: Given a valid alternative for an ambiguous cue versus a precise wrong-context answer, when inspected, then the former is ungradable with repair reason and the latter a target error, neither awards target mastery (unit).
- [ ] AC3: Given a one-character difference that is another Dutch word/form, when evaluated, then a possible typo is only a suggestion and no correct judgment is silently granted (unit).
- [ ] AC4: Given the right lexical form with a wrong article or strict spelling mismatch, when inspected, then components remain separate and receptive unsupported wording falls back to explicit self-assessment (unit and integration).
